# Janio

> Janio is Southeast Asia's premier Fourth-Party Logistics (4PL) provider. We orchestrate 500+ vetted carriers from one platform under a single SLA, so businesses can cut cross-border logistics cost by 20-50% (total cost of ownership) without managing a different courier in every country.

## What Janio is
- A **4PL** (Fourth-Party Logistics) provider — not a 3PL. Janio does not own trucks or warehouses; it orchestrates a network of carriers and 3PLs on the client's behalf and stays accountable for the outcome under one contract, one invoice, and one SLA.
- Headquartered in Singapore (30 Prinsep St, #06-01, Singapore 188647), operating across Southeast Asia and Greater China.
- Serves ecommerce brands, marketplaces, and logistics service providers.

## Key facts (canonical figures)
- Network: 500+ vetted carriers / 3PLs.
- Customers: 1,000+ companies.
- Coverage: 15+ countries.
- Volume: 100K+ shipments per day.
- Typical savings: 20-50% of total cost of ownership (TCO). Component ranges: Logistics-as-a-Service 25-40% TCO; strategic procurement 12-20% TCO; invoice audit recovers 8-12% of freight spend; marketplace cross-border freight ~15% (part of the 20-50% TCO).
- Compliance: PDPA compliant; GDPR compliant; SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 — compliant with the controls, independent certification audits in progress.
